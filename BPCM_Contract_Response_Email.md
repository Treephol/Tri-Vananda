## A. Master Services Agreement

### 1. Clause 2.b.i — Please remove the 17% expense mark-up

We ask that the standard mark-up of 17% on out-of-pocket expenses be removed, and that all expenses be reimbursed at cost, supported by receipts or third-party invoices.

Our position is that the monthly retainer and the tools and database fee already compensate BPCM for the servicing of these expenses; a mark-up on top of both is difficult for us to justify internally.

### 2. Clause 2.b.i — Please remove the business-class travel entitlement

We ask that the entitlement for senior executives to travel business class be removed, and that all air travel serviced to the account be economy class unless Tri Vananda approves an upgrade in writing in advance.

### 3. Clause 2.b.i — Please reduce the prior-approval threshold to $500, and add a monthly aggregate cap

We ask that the threshold requiring our prior approval be reduced from $1,000 to **$500** for any single expense.

We would also ask you to add a monthly aggregate cap: total unapproved out-of-pocket expenditure not to exceed **$1,000 per calendar month** without our prior written approval. This is simply so that a series of individually small items cannot accumulate without visibility on either side.

Please note this change must also be carried into **SOW #1, Section 1**, which currently reads "Any expense over $1000 shall require your advance approval." Clause 1.a provides that the SOW controls in the event of conflict, so unless both are amended the SOW figure would prevail.

### 4. Clause 2.b.ii — Please increase the Advance Expense to $3,000

We ask that the Advance Expense threshold be raised from $1,500 to **$3,000**, so that routine servicing costs do not require a separate advance request each time.

### 5. Clause 2.c — Please qualify the on-site Influencer & Celebrity Relations requirement

As drafted, this clause obliges Tri Vananda to have a member of BPCM's Influencer & Celebrity Relations team on site at every activation involving talent, with travel, accommodation and per diem at our cost, and appears to leave the number of attendees and the level of cost at BPCM's discretion.

We accept the principle where BPCM is the contracting party for the talent. We ask that the clause be qualified in two respects: that it applies only to activations where BPCM has engaged the talent on our behalf, and that all associated travel, accommodation and per diem costs are subject to our **prior written approval on a per-activation basis**, against a budget agreed before any commitment is made.

We would also note that this clause needs to sit consistently with SOW #1, Section 05, which excludes event production, celebrity procurement and paid influencers from the retainer scope.

*Proposed insertion:* "Such on-site presence shall apply only to activations where BPCM is the contracting party for the relevant talent, and all associated travel, accommodation and per diem costs shall be subject to Client's prior written approval and an agreed budget."

### 6. Clause 7 — Please add a notification obligation on third-party permissions

As drafted, Tri Vananda — and not BPCM — bears responsibility and cost for all third-party releases, permissions and rights, including where BPCM identifies, manages or engages the third party in question.

We can accept the allocation of cost. We ask that you add an obligation on BPCM to notify us in advance of any third-party rights that will be required, and to obtain our written approval before incurring or committing us to any such cost. We should not carry liability for permissions we were never made aware of.

### 7. Clause 8.b — Please extend the BPCM indemnity

We ask that the indemnity at Clause 8.b be extended to cover **"or any infringement arising from BPCM services."**

This is consistent with BPCM's own representation at Clause 10.o.ii that all Work Products will not infringe any third-party right, and simply aligns the indemnity with the warranty already given.

## B. Statement of Work #1

### 8. Section 1 — Please remove the automatic renewal

We ask that the automatic renewal for successive six (6) month Renewal Terms be removed. We would prefer that any continuation beyond the Minimum Term be a positive decision taken by both parties rather than a default.

Please note that once this is removed, the surviving termination sentence still refers to "the start of the next Renewal Term," which will no longer have a defined meaning. We would ask you to replace it with the language proposed at Item 10 below.

### 9. Section 1 — Please remove the 7% annual increase

We ask that the provision increasing the monthly fee by 7% on each annual renewal be removed. We would rather review fees openly at the end of each term against the results delivered than have an automatic escalation built into the agreement.

### 10. Section 1 — Please add a six-month break right

This is our most important commercial request.

We are committing to a twelve (12) month Minimum Term with a minimum value of $84,000, paid in advance of services, on a programme whose outcomes are by nature earned rather than guaranteed. In return we ask for a single, clearly defined break right at the mid-point.

*Proposed language:* "Notwithstanding the Minimum Term, Client may terminate this SOW effective at the end of the sixth (6th) month of service by providing BPCM with not less than sixty (60) days' prior written notice. In such event, Client's obligation shall be limited to fees and approved expenses accrued through the effective date of termination, and BPCM shall deliver all Materials together with a full handover of media relationships and pending editorial opportunities. Following the Minimum Term, this SOW shall continue on a month-to-month basis unless either party gives thirty (30) days' written notice."

I want to be clear that this does not reflect any hesitation about BPCM's capability. It is a governance requirement for a first-year engagement with a new international partner, and it gives both sides a natural point at which to review fit and recalibrate.

### 11. Section 1 — Tools and database fee (accepted)

We accept the tools and database fee equal to 4.25% of the retainer ($297.50 per month) as drafted, covering media targeting and monitoring, measurement and analytics, communications platforms and digital subscriptions.

For our internal budgeting we are recording the total annual commitment as **$87,570** ($84,000 retainer plus $3,570 tools and database fee), exclusive of out-of-pocket expenses. Please confirm this matches your understanding.

### 12. Section 1 — Please amend the initial payment provision

We ask that the requirement for $14,000 representing the first and last month of service be restated so that the **last-month payment is held as a credit and applied against the final month of service, whenever this SOW ends** — including where it terminates at the six-month break point under Item 10.

Subject to that clarification, we are content to settle $14,000 in advance of the SOW Effective Date, with subsequent monthly retainer invoices settled in advance of each month of service.

### 13. Section 1 — Please add a withholding right for non-performance

We ask that you add: "The Client reserves the right to withhold any retainer fees in the event that BPCM does not fulfil its duties under this Agreement."

Our intent is not to create an open-ended payment discretion, and we are happy for it to be tied to a process — exercisable only following written notice and a thirty (30) day cure period, mirroring Clause 3.b of the MSA. If you would prefer to draft it that way, we would welcome it.

### 14. Sections 3.I and 04 — Please align the status meeting cadence

There is an inconsistency between the two sections. Section 3.I provides for **bi-weekly** status meetings; Section 04 provides for **weekly** status meetings.

Our preference is weekly status calls, with Section 3.I corrected accordingly. If your staffing model is built around a bi-weekly cadence, please tell us and we will align to bi-weekly in both places — but the two sections should not contradict one another in the executed document.

### 15. Section 05 — Exclusions (accepted)

We accept the exclusions as drafted, including travel and accommodation, photography and videography, crisis communications and social media management. We understand these sit outside the retainer and will be scoped and quoted separately if and when required.
