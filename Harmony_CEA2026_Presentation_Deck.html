  • ResidencesResidential villas designed for multigenerational living, set within nature.
  • WellnessThe Health Resort by Clinique La Prairie — the Swiss pioneer of longevity.

Why Tri Vananda at Dragonfly

Harmony · Discover your Trust Frequency

Trust has a frequency. We built a way to find yours.

Trust is the foundation of Tri Vananda — and of every healthy team, family and community. Yet at leadership summits it stays a word on a slide. So we turned to the beehive: a hive thrives not because its bees are the same, but because seven different roles find rhythm together. From that truth came seven Trust Frequencies — the distinct way each of us gives and earns trust.

  • The MazeThe Bee Maze — a walk-through game that turned trust from something you hear about into something you do.
  • The RevealA personal frequency card — Forager, Nurse, Architect, Queen's Whisperer, Pollinator, Sentinel or Harmonizer.
  • The BarJAMPA Bar — a botanical drink composed to your tune, and a conversation that flowed naturally to the sales gallery.

Harmony is trust in motion.

The centerpiece · Trust Frequency Bee Maze

A game you walk through. A mirror you leave with.

Eight hexagon rooms, built like cells of a hive. Guests entered with an empty basket and moved room to room — playing, choosing, collecting tokens. No right answers, no score. At the end, the tokens poured into bowls and an archetype appeared: recognition, not judgment.

  • Earn trustTexture of Trust · Path of Trust · Flower of Choice · Echo of Trust — four rooms on credibility and reliability.
  • Give trustPlant a Word · Face of Trust · Your Intimacy Space · Gesture of Trust — four rooms on openness and care.
  • Leave withYour Trust Frequency — a card, a matched JAMPA drink, and a language for how you connect.

The Reveal

A sample of the result.

THE NURSE
Safe · Emotionally present
This frequency is safe and emotionally present. They build trust by showing up with consistent care and empathy, earning deep trust over time through their steady presence and quiet support. While they may not always lead from the front, their strength is felt in the spaces they nurture. Their Trust Gift is Care, and their Leadership Cue is to remember that their quiet consistency is powerful, emphasizing the impact of small, steady acts.
  • Gives trustTo those who show care through experience, not theory — who nurture trust through small, steady actions, show empathy without judgment, and give consistent, nurturing support.
  • Earns trustBy sharing knowledge with care and empathy, building trust gradually, and nurturing it through care and presence — emotionally present, steady, quietly supportive.
  • In leadershipTrusts leaders who are the heart of an operation — remembering details, showing support, balancing care with clarity and accountability.
  • CueQuiet consistency is powerful — never underestimate small, steady acts. Balance nurturing support with clear communication and, when necessary, accountability: boundaries and honest feedback show you care enough to ensure growth.
