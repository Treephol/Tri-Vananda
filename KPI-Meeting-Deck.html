Part Three · U.S. PR Research · The Brief

Reach top-tier media —
and turn audiences into sales.

Tri Vananda needs a U.S.-anchored partner that can do two things at once — and few agencies truly can: reach high-net-worth American buyers through the trusted earned media they read, and convert a wellness community into residence sales.

There are two kinds of agency — those built for travel and hospitality, who win coverage and bookings, and those built for real estate, who sell units. The right partner does both.

01The closer · residence sales

Relevance International

New York · LA · London · Dubai · woman-owned · real estate & branded residences

Relevance is the one that lives and breathes real estate. They're a New York firm built specifically to sell branded residences to wealthy buyers around the world — and they have the receipts. When they launched Fendi Château Residences in Miami, more than 80% of the homes sold, with around US$70M in a single month after the Art Basel event. In Athens, The Ellinikon sold out before anyone broke ground.

Clients: Fendi Château Residences · Atlantis The Royal Residences · 111 Murray · The Setai · Porto Montenegro.
Why they fit: they reach exactly the U.S. titles our buyers read, and they can prove that coverage turns into sell-through.
What to watch: they lead with property, so we'd need to brief the wellness soul in hard — and check how deep they really go across Asia-Pacific media.
Fendi Château Residences
02The natural fit · wellness story

J/PR

9 U.S. offices + London · founded 2005 · luxury travel, hospitality & development

J/PR is our storyteller. They're a big luxury-travel and hospitality name, but the reason they're on this list is Velvære in Park City. It's the closest thing out there to what we're building — a wellness residential community, told as a real-estate story to American buyers, with coverage in Robb Report and Forbes that turned into actual home reservations. They also pulled more than 55 million impressions for The Lakes by Yoo.

Clients: Dorchester Collection · Waldorf Astoria New York · Pendry · Relais & Châteaux · Canouan Private Estates.
Why they fit: the best blend of wellness credibility and U.S. firepower — and the only firm here who's done a wellness-residential launch just like ours.
What to watch: they've historically chased bookings, so we'd set residence reservations and sales as the KPIs from day one.
Velvære · Park City
03The soul-keeper · wellness & spa

Victoria King PR

Los Angeles · founded 1992 · boutique · luxury resorts, hotels, spas & travel

Victoria King is the boutique with the deepest wellness instincts. She founded her L.A. firm back in 1992 and has spent more than three decades representing some of the most beautiful resorts, hotels and spas in the world — places like Ballyfin in Ireland and Manoir Hovey in Canada. Before all that she ran PR for the Bel-Air Hotel Company. This is high-touch, relationship-led work: she knows the travel and lifestyle editors personally.

Clients: Ballyfin (Ireland) · Manoir Hovey (Canada) · Mas d'en Bruno (Spain) · Winnetu Oceanside Resort · Angsana Resorts.
Why they fit: nobody on this list understands wellness and spa storytelling better, and that editor trust protects how our brand feels.
What to watch: she's hospitality-first, not a residence-sales shop, and a boutique — so we'd pair her with a real-estate partner if villa sell-through is the priority, and check her bandwidth.
