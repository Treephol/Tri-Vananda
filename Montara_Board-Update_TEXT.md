## 03 · The Brand

**Heading:** PATAMA · Branding
**Headline:** A story only *this* family can tell.

**Why:**
The weaving idea is still being developed, but it is not ours alone yet — change the name and the same story would still work. So we go back to the real decisions the family made, and what they gave up to make them, and build the brand on a belief no one else can copy.

**Items:**
- Brand & Logo
- Guidelines
- Coffee Table Book Template

**Where we are & what happens next:**
We are gathering the evidence now: filmed conversations with the family, then interviews across the company about the decisions that built Montara. A workshop turns that evidence into one clear belief. Design starts only after that — the identity first, then the guidelines that make it usable, and the book last, because the book needs a finished brand.

## 04 · The Product

**Heading:** PATAMA · Marketing Collateral
**Headline:** One product. One *promise*.

**Why:**
We are not waiting for PATAMA to be perfect before we sell it. One paid product, sold and measured honestly, will tell us whether PATAMA creates value we would not have had anyway.

**Items:**
- PATAMA Family Journey — Living Well Together
- PATAMA Website

**Where we are & what happens next:**
We are writing the journey now — what a family actually does during a stay, and what it is worth. The brochure follows the content, and the website follows the brochure. We start with families we already know, and spend on reaching new people later.

---

## 05 · The Summit

**Heading:** Montara × Global Wellness Summit
**Headline:** Four things we do with the *Summit*.

**Why:**
The Summit turns twenty this year, and it comes to our island. Its theme — the Science, Art and Soul of wellness — matches our own story. We proposed a package built together with the Summit, so each part of the theme is answered by one of our properties.

**Items:**
- Brand awareness of the Montara Group
- A hosted farm-to-table dinner at Tri Vananda
- Montara experiences within official GWS tours
- Leadership participation & narrative positioning

**Where we are & what happens next:**
The four areas are agreed in principle, and the tour route is agreed between Tri Vananda, Clinique La Prairie and Trisara. Invitations come next: a guest list for each dinner, then the final programme with the organiser. Everything on the estate has to be finished before the first guest arrives.

---

## 06 · PR & Communications

**Heading:** Tri Vananda · PR & Communications
**Headline:** One story. Two *markets*.

**Why:**
The Summit gives us one moment. The agencies make it last. One tells the residence story abroad, to buyers who have never set foot in Phuket. The other builds our name at home, where the project is judged first. Coverage runs before the Summit, during it, and long after — so trust builds instead of spiking once and fading.

**Items:**
- PR Agency · US · BPCM
- PR Agency · TH · Vivaldi

**Where we are & what happens next:**
The US agreement is in final review, and the Thai agency has sent its proposal. We expect to appoint both this month. Briefing starts straight away, so the plan is agreed and coverage has already begun when the Summit opens.

## 07 · The Residence

**Heading:** Tri Vananda · Sales & Enquiry Tools
**Headline:** The residence is the *point*.

**Why:**
PATAMA, Clinique La Prairie and Jampa are not separate announcements. They are the proof of why owning here is different from owning a villa anywhere else. We are selling the residence, not the wellness brand.

**Items:**
- PATAMA Residence CGIs
- Quarterly Newsletter
- Always-On Digital Marketing

**Where we are & what happens next:**
A first set of images is going for approval, then into final production, ready for conversations during the Summit. The newsletter is being written and will bring the first residence and Clinique La Prairie stories to the people we already know. Always-on digital keeps the story going in between, and sends every enquiry into one pipeline.

## 08 · A Place Already Alive

**Heading:** Activation 03 & 06
**Headline:** A place already *alive*.

**Why:**
The most convincing thing a buyer can see is ordinary life happening. Each gathering leads to the next, and viewings happen inside a real community, not beside it.

**Items:**

- Bi-Weekly Local Roots

**Where we are & what happens next:**
