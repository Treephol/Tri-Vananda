A Thai family’s idea of stewardship.

Montara Hospitality Group is a Thai family-owned hospitality group and the owner and developer of some of Phuket’s most personal luxury destinations. Since opening Trisara in 2004, the family has been guided by the belief that hospitality is a responsibility to care for the land, the community, and future generations.

From luxury hospitality
to wellness living.

The garden in the third heaven.

Trisara takes its name from Sanskrit and has been Montara Hospitality Group’s flagship since 2004. Terraced into a forested hillside within a protected nature preserve on a private bay, each pool villa, suite, and residence faces the Andaman Sea and its sunsets.

Privacy is the real luxury here — a private bay, villas terraced so each keeps its own horizon, and a pool at every door. Service is quietly anticipatory, closer to being hosted by a family than checked into a hotel. Wellbeing is offered as an art rather than a programme, and PRU, the group’s MICHELIN-starred restaurant, sits within the grounds.

The restaurant grows only when the community does.

At PRU, sustainability and community are not initiatives — they are how the restaurant lives each day. The all-electric kitchen avoids fossil fuels; what must be grilled is grilled over its own wood and charcoal.

Chef Jimmy works alongside the fishermen, farmers and makers he buys from, guiding a young cacao farmer, backing those who fish sustainably, so that the restaurant’s growth becomes theirs. Artisanal craft runs through the whole experience, from the chairs to the tiles to the art on the walls.

A place to live well, across generations.

Tri Vananda is a wellness community in the north of Phuket — a place to live, not simply to visit, where families can be well together across generations.

It sits within 237 acres of forest, lakes and farmland, and most of that land will always stay as it is. Homes are built around gardens, daylight and fresh air. Days are shaped by walking, growing food, sharing meals and being outdoors. Clinique La Prairie Phuket sits within its grounds.

Swiss longevity, in a Phuket forest.

Clinique La Prairie is a Swiss health clinic founded in 1931 on the shores of Lake Geneva, known for helping people live longer, healthier lives.

In November 2026 it opens its first health resort in Asia, set within the forests and lakes of Tri Vananda. Every guest follows a personal plan built around four things: medical care, nutrition, movement and wellbeing — Swiss expertise, delivered with Thai warmth.
