What the name must carry

Born from ten years of PRU.
Built to outgrow them.

One master name for a permanent annual platform: five nights of collaborative dinners at PRU, and a farm gathering of twenty chefs and 150 guests at Tri Vananda — presented by PRU, returning every year.

  • FeelDiscovery without saying it. The philosophy performed, not announced.
  • HoldTwo rooms at once. Nights of collaborative dinners and a food festival in the farm.
  • LastEditions, like vintages. The anniversary is year one's theme — never the name.
  • TravelInternational mouth-feel. Easy in London, Tokyo, Sydney — unmistakably of Phuket.
PRU · Plant · Raise · Understand
The idea beneath all three

Food is the shortest distance between us and the world.

Every dish begins somewhere we cannot see — in soil and rain, in the hands that planted and raised it. To taste with attention is to discover: the land and what it remembers, the people beside us, the senses we had stopped noticing, and a fuller way of being alive. Each candidate name below is a different door into this same idea.

The place · The movement · The statement

Candidate 01 · The Place
UNDERSTORY

un·der·sto·ry · noun · the living layer beneath the forest canopy, where regeneration begins — and, read again, the story underneath: the hidden narrative inside every ingredient, every place, every person at the table.

Discovery as depth,
not distance.

Understory · The logic

The richest layer is the one you have to look for.

  • FoodFlavour literally lives beneath the surface — roots, broths, ferments, terroir. A chef's work is excavation.
  • PRUPlant · Raise · Understand is a method of going below the obvious. Ten years of digging deeper into Thai ingredients.
  • Tri VanandaVana means forest — and the understory is where a forest renews itself. The Farm is a soil-first place.
  • PATAMAWonder, Growth, Sanctuary: a fuller life is found beneath the surface of ordinary days.
  • Long runInexhaustible editorial depth — each edition peels back a new layer: soil, water, roots, night.

Candidate 02 · The Movement
FURTHER

fur·ther · adverb · beyond the point already reached. The one-word answer to an anniversary: not a look back at ten years, but the promise that the exploration has only begun.

Discovery as a direction,
not a destination.

Further · The logic

Ten years on — further still.

  • FoodHow far can an ingredient go? Every collaboration pushes a flavour past its known edge.
  • PRUA decade of refusing to be satisfied — experimentation as posture, celebrated by continuing it.
  • Tri VanandaThe festival literally goes further afield — out of the dining room, into the land.
  • PATAMAGrowth and Becoming: a life that keeps moving toward a fuller version of itself.
  • Watch-outAbstract on its own — no food, no place in the word. The programme names do the anchoring, and the lockup with PRU matters more than for the other two.
The horizon line · Tri Vananda
Candidate 03 · The Statement
THE DISCOVERY
FEAST

the dis·cov·er·y feast · noun · a feast held in the name of finding — new ingredients, new people, new perspectives. The philosophy said in plain words, and “The” claiming it as the one that matters.

Discovery as an open invitation,
understood everywhere.

The Discovery Feast · The logic

The philosophy, said out loud.

  • FoodFeast is the most generous word in food — abundance, occasion, a table with room for everyone. Warmer and more premium than “fest.”
  • PRUThe direct heir of A Decade of Discovery — ten years of discovery through food, carried forward instead of closed.
  • Tri VanandaA feast held on the land itself — long tables, open sky, the farm as host.
  • PATAMAWonder and Growth stated literally — discovery is the promise, the feast is how it's kept.
  • Watch-outIt says the philosophy rather than performs it. “Discovery” is category vocabulary — hardest of the three to own and protect over a decade.
