PATAMA Wellness at Tri Vananda · Master Plan

A wellness club,
within the forest.

The PATAMA facilities, gathered along the estate's central spine.

The first look at PATAMA Wellness at Tri Vananda, designed by HABITA Architects within the Montara Wellness framework. The wellness facilities organise along the estate's central spine — anchored by the lake, set within regenerated forest, and held in dialogue with the existing homes. Each building is low, biophilic, and turned toward the trees. Together they form PATAMA Wellness at Tri Vananda — a private membership club within nature.
