PR Agency Shortlist

Choosing a U.S. communications partner to sell the villas and carry the PATAMA ecosystem story.
Internal strategy · 29 May 2026 · Use ← → to navigate
The brief

Two jobs, at once.

The right partner has to do two different things well — and most agencies only do one.

  • Reach HNWI buyers in the U.S. through the earned media they trust — NYT, WSJ, FT, Robb Report, broadcast.
  • Convert a wellness community into villa sales — residence PR that moves units, not just hotel coverage.
  • Carry the larger story — the PATAMA ecosystem and net-zero positioning, told without overstating.
How we evaluated

Four criteria, weighted to your priorities

  • U.S. media & HNWI reach — primary
  • Luxury real estate & branded residences — primary
  • Wellness & hospitality fit — supporting
  • Ecosystem & sustainability storytelling — supporting

Scored 1–5 from each agency's own published case studies and client lists. Directional — meant to frame conversations, not replace a chemistry meeting.

Benchmark · Discover Collection

Sell belonging, and the villas follow.

  • Lead with community, not square footage. Buyers join a multigenerational well-living community; the real estate is the means.
  • Wellbeing & sustainability as proof. Net-zero botanical setting and longevity programming are the real differentiators.
  • Earned trust over hard sell. Build desire through story, press and experience that reach HNWI buyers — then convert privately.
Next steps

  • 5 · Test the PATAMA / net-zero story without overstating.
  • 6 · Hold chemistry meetings before any paid scoping.
