iBreadth & fitOne retainer covers media relations, social, influencer/KOL, content & partnerships — enough to run several 2026–27 initiatives without multiple vendors.
iiKnows our worldAlready represents Trisara with the deepest wellness portfolio of the three — a materially shorter ramp than a new agency.
iiiPR-to-sales proofVelvære produced seven villa reservations from earned media before launch — the closest analogue to selling villas, not accumulating impressions.
ivModern measurementMeltwater + AI/LLM-visibility lens and Barcelona Principles give the board credible, current accountability.
vSensible economicsAt $6,860/mo (admin, tech, monitoring + one annual FAM included), it delivers integrated capability at a measured, defensible cost.
The alternative · Victoria King PR

VKPR remains the value and relationship option: the lowest fee, senior hands-on attention, and an existing Trisara relationship. It is the right pick if the board prefers a lean, PR-only specialist and will handle social, influencer and creative elsewhere — a simpler, more economical engagement than the “one partner, several workstreams” route.

Trade-offs we accept

For J/PR, some elements — media missions, ambassador programs and expanded influencer/social — are scoped and billed separately; fix a capped annual envelope at contracting. J/PR is U.S.-led, so agree a defined renewal review at the one-year mark to reassess reach as buyer geographies become clearer.

This recommendation is based solely on the written proposals and is intended to support — not replace — the board’s deliberation.

Appendix
What are the Barcelona Principles?
Referenced in J/PR’s reporting model
AMEC · established 2010 · updated 2015 & 2020

The Barcelona Principles are a global set of standards for measuring PR, communications and reputation, developed by AMEC — the International Association for the Measurement and Evaluation of Communication — since 2010. They exist because PR was traditionally reported as raw volume, prompting the CEO’s inevitable question: “So how does that actually help the business?”

Why they emerged — the old way
  • 100 pieces of coverage
  • Reach of 50 million
  • Media value of ฿20M
  • AVE of ฿30M
“So how does that help the business?” — the question every board asks back.
The shift in thinking
“How much are we talked about?”
→
“What results does our communication create for the business?”

Every measure is anchored to a business objective — not to visibility for its own sake.

The Core Principles
1
Outputs ≠ Outcomes ≠ Impact

The heart of the framework. What you publish is not the same as what people come to think, which is not the same as what the business gains.

LevelMeaningTri Vananda example
OutputWhat we produce80 pieces of coverage across APAC
OutcomeHow people’s perception changesTVND seen as a Longevity Community, not just luxury villas
ImpactThe business resultSite visits up 20%
2
Communication must have a clear objective

Before a campaign, you must answer “Why are we doing this?” — because without an objective there is nothing to measure against.

Weak

“We want lots of PR.”

Strong

“Build awareness that Tri Vananda is a leader in Longevity Living in Asia.”

3
Measure quality, not just quantity

100 pieces are not automatically better than 20. What matters is message pull-through, audience relevance, sentiment and the authority of the outlet.

CoverageValue
50 small blogsLow
A single feature in SCMPVery high
4
AVE is not a valid measure

AVE (Advertising Value Equivalency) claims a piece of coverage “would have cost ฿1M as an ad.” The Barcelona Principles hold that PR is not advertising and should not be measured like it — which is why AVE has been abandoned by major organisations worldwide.

5
Measure both qualitative and quantitative
Quantitative
  • Coverage count
  • Reach
  • Engagement
  • Website traffic
Qualitative
  • Do people understand the brand correctly?
  • Do journalists use our key messages?
  • Does AI understand our positioning?
6
Measure every channel together

The lines between PR, SEO, social and AI are disappearing. A buyer may see coverage → search Google → ask ChatGPT → visit the website → book a site visit. It must be measured as a single ecosystem, not as silos.

7
Measurement must be transparent and verifiable

Every number should explain where it comes from, how it was measured, and what its limitations are.

Traditional PR Report

  • Reach of 20 million
  • 95% positive sentiment

Barcelona Principles Report

Outcome

  • Search volume for “Longevity Living Thailand” up 40%
  • AI begins associating PATAMA with the category
Impact

  • Site visits +10% · 2 additional units closed
Traditional PR asks…Barcelona Principles ask…
How often are we mentioned?What did we change?
How much reach?What do people now understand?
What is the AVE?What did the business gain?
CoverageBusiness impact

In one sentence: the Barcelona Principles shift PR measurement from “how many people might see it” to the real impact on thinking, behaviour and business.
