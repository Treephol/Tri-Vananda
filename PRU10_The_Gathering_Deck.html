What The Gathering means

One gathering, grown from PRU's three.

PRU has always stood for Plant · Raise · Understand. The Gathering brings all three to one table — a week of special collaborations with great chefs from restaurants that share the same philosophy, cooking side by side in Phuket.

01 · Plant

Gathered from the Land

Deeper care for nature and everything we plant — the soil, the seed and the seasons of Thailand, tended with a lighter hand each year.
02 · Raise

Gathered with the Makers

Side by side with the chefs of kindred restaurants — and the farmers, fishers, producers and makers behind everything we raise. All named, celebrated and at the table with us.
03 · Understand

Gathered to Create

The curiosity and knowledge to understand every ingredient more deeply — and the creativity to take it somewhere it has never been.
