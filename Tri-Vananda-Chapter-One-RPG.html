◆ CHAPTER 01 ◆
A NEW HOME
UNDER THE
MANGO TREES
"You have been on the road a long time.
Your new life is one tree ahead."
◆ NARRATOR
The road has been winding for a long time. Your sister Ploy is asleep beside you — mouth a little open, one sandal fallen off. Outside, fields of green turn into forest, and forest turns into something you can't quite name yet.
▼
▸ FATHER
We're here. Look up.
▼
◆ NARRATOR
You step out of the car. And the first thing that happens is — you forget to breathe. The air smells green. It smells wet. Somewhere, a bird sings a note like a tiny bell. Somewhere else, far off, water moves. You stand there, and your shoulders drop without you noticing.
▼
◆ CHOOSE A PATH
  • Go inside and find your new room The window upstairs opens right into the tree...
  • Step into the back garden Something small and green is waiting in the grass...
▸ PLOY
Nara! Come and see! I think... he is trying to tell me something.
◆ You found TUA-TUA the frog.
▼
◆ NARRATOR
Your window looks straight into the tree. Close enough to touch. A butterfly the colour of old gold drifts past. And then — down in the garden — something moves. Something green, hopping. And behind it, something bigger. A shadow that isn't quite a bird. It is there, and then it isn't.
▼
▸ ARUN
You're new. I'm Arun. This is for you. ...Do you like lakes? There are nine. I can show you — tomorrow, early. Meet me at the big tamarind.
◆ Got: ONE WARM MANGO.
▼
◆ WHAT DO YOU DO?
  • Run after Arun — find the tamarind now His bike is fading into the forest. If you hurry...
  • Stay with the mango tree Something about this tree. You want to sit a while.
◆ NARRATOR
A leaf lets go from somewhere very high, and spirals down, and lands — softly — on top of your head. Arun grins. "That means the tree likes you." You look at the leaf. There are tiny marks on it. Almost like writing. Almost like — a riddle.
▼
◆ NARRATOR
The bark is warm against your back. You close your eyes. And the tree — the whole tree — breathes. A slow in. A slow out. Bigger than yours. Then, as if it had been aiming, a leaf lets go from above. It lands in your lap. You look at it. There are tiny marks on it. Almost like writing. Almost like — a riddle.
▼
▸ PLOY
Look. Tua-Tua dropped this... I think. (She holds out a leaf.) Nara, your leaf... and my leaf... they're the same leaf.
▸ NARA: "We go."
