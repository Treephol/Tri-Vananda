Tri Vananda · The Project
A sanctuary for wellbeing & nature at the heart of the island.

Tri Vananda · The New Wellness Brand
New wellness brand under development.

Across the Montara family of properties, wellness will take three distinct forms — prescribed at Clinique La Prairie, lived at Tri Vananda, and celebrated at Trisara. On their own, each would be told as a separate story. A new wellness brand will bring them together, so the whole reads not as parts, but as a single, coherent way of living.

Three expressions of wellness across the estate
Longevity prescribed
Health Resort by Clinique La Prairie
Clinical longevity and preventive medicine — credentialed by Switzerland’s most established medical-wellness brand.
Long life lived
New Development at Tri Vananda
Wellbeing as a daily practice — the new facilities and residential community at the heart of the estate.
Life celebrated
Renovated Trisara Wellness
Wellbeing at the beachfront — twenty years of luxury hospitality, renewed under the new brand.

Stronger together than apart: longevity prescribed, a long life lived, and life celebrated — one continuous arc, from the clinic to the everyday to the shoreline. A single wellness brand will carry that arc across every property, turning a collection of places into one ecosystem — a story each new venue can join, and one no single property could tell alone.
