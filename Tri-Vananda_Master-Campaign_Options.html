01

Why now. The season everything arrives.

Between September and November 2026, the group's whole thesis becomes visible at once: PATAMA Wellness launches at Trisara, Clinique La Prairie opens its health resort, PRU hosts the Farm Festival, and the Global Wellness Summit brings the industry's most influential people to our door.

Today these read as separate events with separate audiences. A master campaign makes them chapters of a single story — so that every tour, dinner, booth and press release compounds instead of competes. The campaign speaks from the Montara / PATAMA perspective: the group as author, the estate as proof.

The framework · Science | Art | Soul
Science

The evidence

Clinique La Prairie's clinical and longevity layer — measurable, credible, globally established. The proof that wellbeing here is real, not rhetorical.

Art

The craft

Trisara's hospitality and PRU's Michelin-starred, farm-driven cuisine — the made things. Wellbeing practised with the care of a craft, not administered as a service.

Soul

The meaning

Tri Vananda and PATAMA — family, nature, community, a practice kept across generations. The reason the science and the craft exist at all.

Every option below runs on this framework. What differs is the angle of entry — which truth leads, and what the audience is asked to feel.

IOption One · The portfolio story

The Weave. All the good things, together.

A life well lived is not one thing — it is many good things, woven together. Only Montara weaves them at one address.

The single insight

People believe wellness demands a choice — the clinic or the beach, discipline or joy, science or pleasure. The truly well never choose. They weave.

The single feeling

Fullness. The quiet completeness of a day where the body, the table, the family and the forest all had their place.

The idea in practice

Drawn directly from the PATAMA manifesto — eight threads, turned on their side, becoming infinity. The campaign follows real days on the estate where Science, Art and Soul cross: a morning diagnostic, a lunch grown fifty metres from the table, an evening with three generations. No single thread makes the story. The weave does.

Signature executions

A campaign film shot as one continuous day across the estate · a woven visual identity system carried through GWS booth, tour collateral and social · "The Weave" editorial series — one thread per month, one real person per thread.

Art

PRU and Trisara carry the sensory chapters — cuisine, hospitality, place.

Soul

PATAMA is the loom — the philosophy holding every thread in tension.

IIOption Two · The category argument

What Are the Years For? Science extends life. PATAMA fills it.

The longevity industry has promised the world more years. It has stayed silent on what fills them. We are the answer to the question longevity forgot to ask.

The single insight

The tension is — billions are spent adding years to life, almost nothing on adding life to years. Every longevity clinic sells the extension. No one owns the content of the extended life.

The single feeling

Recognition. The exhale of hearing someone finally say the thing you had quietly suspected about the longevity race.

The idea in practice

A point-of-view campaign timed to the moment CLP's science arrives on our estate. We embrace the science completely — then complete it. The clinic gives you the years; the farm, the table, the family and the forest are what the years are for. Editorial-first: essays, talks and one provocation put directly to the wellness industry at GWS.

Signature executions

A manifesto film asking one question — "What are the years for?" · founder essay placed in tier-one media at CLP opening · GWS keynote / panel positioning Montara as the philosophical counterweight · print series pairing one clinical fact with one lived answer.

IIIOption Three · The origin story

A Standard Kept. One family's practice, opened to the world.

PATAMA is a family name and a long-held practice. This is not a brand launched — it is a standard kept for generations, now opened to those who share it.

The single insight

People believe what a family does for itself over what a company sells to strangers. In a category crowded with invented wellness brands, provenance is the rarest asset — and ours is real.

The single feeling

Trust. The sense of being invited into something that existed long before you — and will outlast you.

The idea in practice

Documentary-led storytelling from the family's own journey — illness, recovery, and the standard that grew from it: rigour in what is built, care in how it is made, meaning in why. Every brand in the portfolio becomes a chapter of one household's practice. Quiet, human, unhurried — the anti-launch. Discovery, never announcement.

Signature executions

A craft-documentary film series (the land, the table, the practice) · founder-told story for U.S. tier-one press — made for the incoming PR partner's mandate · "kept standards" content pillar: real people, real dates, real proof · intimate hosted dinners as the campaign's true media.

A note on choosing

These are not three campaigns.
They are one story at three altitudes.

The Weave is the brand platform — the long-term architecture. What Are the Years For? is the 2026 campaign — the sharp, newsworthy expression timed to CLP and GWS. A Standard Kept is the proof layer — the origin story that makes both believable, and the natural brief for the incoming U.S. PR partner.

The recommendation: choose the lead altitude for Sep–Nov 2026, knowing the other two can nest beneath it. If forced to one: Option II for this season — it converts the calendar we already have into an argument only we can make.
