04 / 07  ·  MICRO-SEGMENT SUMMARY
04
Step Four  ·  Summarise Micro-Segments for the Campaigns

From many possible people to two we will speak to.

What we did

Reviewed all GTM options and identified the most promising segments. Enriched them with real-world data and shaped them into clear GTM directions for campaign development. Then planned the marketing campaign based on the selected GTM — key channels and a supporting list of required collateral.

Result

Refined audience targeting for both campaigns — two buyers, two psychologies, one property. Ready to become two parallel campaigns with opposite creative temperatures.

C1 — the legacy buyer.
C2 — the recognition buyer.

05 / 07  ·  DIGITAL CAMPAIGN PLANNING
05
Step Five  ·  Digital Campaign Planning & Key Messages

Same funnel. Opposite temperatures.

Two parallel Meta Ads campaigns. Same funnel — Ad → LP → WhatsApp → Visit. Same budget split — THB 50K each / 30 days. Opposite buyer psychologies. Opposite creative temperatures.

C1  ·  Business Tycoon with Family

Connoisseur / Achiever — legacy and longevity

Age 35+  ·  HK, Singapore, Bangkok, Shanghai, Sydney, Melbourne
Stage
Permission — "Is this the right operating base for the next chapter of my family?"
Insight
The wealthy aren't moving for tax. They're moving for environments that match the standard they hold internally.
Idea
Where Asia's wealth is moving — and why now.
Feeling
The quiet relief of recognition — this is the place I have been looking for.
Objective
Traffic → LP Views (switch to Lead at 400+ events)
Targeting
Wellness · Luxury Real Estate · Intl. Travellers · Family & Wealth · 1% LAL
Tone
Editorial, family-led, high-context, never selling.
"Not everything you own should feel the same" "Where your time starts behaving differently"
Forecast: 20,000–33,000 LP visits · 50–80 WA chats · 1–2 qualified visits
C2  ·  Impulse Buyer + Thailand Partners

Achiever / Experientialist with a Curator's eye

Age 35–55  ·  Phuket, Phang Nga  ·  Bangkok (Sukhumvit, Sathorn, Thonglor)
Stage
Desire ↔ Recognition — the moment a property stops being a property and becomes the version of life they've been quietly designing in their head.
Insight
They're not shopping for square metres. They're shopping for the version of themselves that already lives there — and they'll know it inside the first thirty seconds.
Idea
The taste you've been building was always pointing here.
Feeling
The slow exhale of recognition — this is what I meant.
Objective
Traffic → LP Views (switch to Lead at 250+ events)
Targeting
Design & Architecture · Luxury Hotels · Wellness · Fine Dining · Modern Art · Couples & Creative Founders · 2–3% LAL from CRM
Daypart
Thu 14:00 – Mon 23:00 — the long-weekend daydreaming window.
Tone
Sensory, lifestyle-led, atmospheric — the ad is a feeling, not an offer. Never explains. Never sells.
"Built for those designing life for the long term" "Your Life, Held Differently"
Forecast: 27,000–50,000 LP visits · 110–220 WA chats · 1–3 qualified visits

06 / 07  ·  LANDING PAGE PRODUCTION (C1)
06
Step Six  ·  Landing Page Production (C1)

Claude produced. The team curated.

The brand is what was chosen to keep. Every step marked Claude is production. Every step marked Tri Vananda Team is judgment — the choice of which brand documents to feed in, which skills to activate, which images to pick, which lines to cut, which version to ship. The landing page exists because both halves of the framework were honoured: the volume of capability, and the discipline of editing.

View the C1 Landing Page → View the C2 Landing Page →
#StepBy
01 Setup Claude with brand documentsUploaded the Tri Vananda brand guideline, master positioning, copy archive, image library, awards, and C1 hero collateral. Tri Vananda Team
02 Upgrade Claude with Luminary skillsInstalled the Luminary 40-skill creative agency system — verbal identity, HNWI psychology, campaign strategy, hospitality branding, frontend design. Converts Claude from a general writer into a calibrated luxury creative director. Tri Vananda Team
03 Creative directionProposed the editorial structure, narrative arc, hero concept, and permission psychology aligned to the C1 buyer. Claude
04 HTML draftBuilt the full single-file landing page — editorial layout, typography system, motion, and component library. Claude
05 Image selectionChose the photography from the brand library — hero, forest, family, community, residences, F&B. Tri Vananda Team
06 Copy refinementRewrote captions, headers, and surrounding paragraphs so the words served the chosen images — not the other way around. Tri Vananda Team
07 Content review against personaRead every line against the C1 persona (Business Tycoon with Family). Cut anything that performed wellness or sold features. Kept only what a tycoon would recognise. Claude
08 Curation & final cutsRead it again. Removed half a dozen things that were almost good but not quite. Approved the version that felt like Tri Vananda, not like a website about Tri Vananda. Tri Vananda Team
