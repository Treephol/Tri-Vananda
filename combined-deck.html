Part 02  ·  The Brand Orchestration Framework

How we built the campaign.

Seven steps. From the lifestyle qualifier to the live Meta ad — shown for C1 and C2.
01
Step One  ·  Workshop & Research

Who are we actually building this for?

We set up the lifestyle qualifier scoring system and conducted research on GTM micro-segment personas based on real-world individuals. Ten lenses — not one. Together they build a single, honest read of the person we're speaking to.

Result

The prompt for the next step.

The ten lifestyle qualifiers

Ten quotients, one portrait. Each letter is a different way of answering the question: does this person already live the way Tri Vananda is built to hold them?

Step Four  ·  Summarise Micro-Segments for the Campaigns

From many possible people to two we will speak to.

What we did

Reviewed all GTM options and identified the most promising segments. Enriched them with real-world data and shaped them into clear GTM directions for campaign development. Then planned the marketing campaign based on the selected GTM — key channels and a supporting list of required collateral.

Result

Refined audience targeting for both campaigns — two buyers, two psychologies, one property. Ready to become two parallel campaigns with opposite creative temperatures.

C1 — the legacy buyer.
C2 — the recognition buyer.

Step Five  ·  Digital Campaign Planning & Key Messages

Same funnel. Opposite temperatures.

Two parallel Meta Ads campaigns. Same funnel — Ad → LP → WhatsApp → Visit. Same budget split — THB 50K each / 30 days. Opposite buyer psychologies. Opposite creative temperatures.

Step Six  ·  Landing Page Production (C1)

Claude produced. The team curated.

The brand is what was chosen to keep. Every step marked Claude is production. Every step marked Tri Vananda Team is judgment — the choice of which brand documents to feed in, which skills to activate, which images to pick, which lines to cut, which version to ship. The landing page exists because both halves of the framework were honoured: the volume of capability, and the discipline of editing.
