The Brief
01

What we are deciding

Three agencies have submitted proposals to lead PR for Tri Vananda — the multigenerational well-living community in Phuket — positioning the villas, PATAMA Wellness and the Clinique La Prairie opening to global HNW buyers.

Our objective
  • Select one partner to support several initiatives this year and next (launch moments through to sales).
  • Translate a complex, multi-part offering into a clear, buyer-ready story.
  • Flexibility to scale and adapt scope is a strong advantage.
What each proposal addresses
  • Residential villa sales · PATAMA wellness brand · Clinique La Prairie launch
  • Media relations, trends, awards, hostings & reporting

Boutique, hands-on PR agency for luxury hotels, resorts and residences. Victoria King personally supervises every account (35+ years). Covers North America from LA, NY, Denver & Miami.

Considerations
  • Narrowest scope — PR only (no social, paid, branding or experiential).
  • North America focus; less global / Asia & Gulf reach.

For Discussion
08

Questions to settle the decision

How many workstreams beyond core PR (social, paid, branding, experiential, CRM) will we run in 2026–2027 — favouring a single full-service partner or a focused PR specialist?
How much weight to give existing Trisara/Montara familiarity (faster ramp, shared media) versus a fresh, category-defining outside perspective?
What is our comfortable annualised budget once expenses, FAMs/media missions and separately-scoped items are included?
Which buyer geographies matter most (North America vs. global incl. Asia & the Gulf) — and whose footprint fits best?
How important is integrated AI-era measurement and influencer capability to our view of “modern” PR?

A full written summary of all three proposals accompanies this deck for detailed reference.
