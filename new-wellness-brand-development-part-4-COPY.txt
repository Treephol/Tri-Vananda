New Wellness Brand Development · The Concept
Three readings of one word, woven into a single practice.
Drawn from Pali, Sanskrit, and Thai, the word patama holds three meanings — and the brand is named for all three at once.
I
The Beginning
LIVE · paṭhama
Living Well Begins Together
The original way of living well — wellbeing integrated into everyday life, the table, the body, the family. Not escape. The starting point.
Pali · paṭhom · first, foremost, the beginning
II
The Lotus
THRIVE · padma
Living Well, Awakening Together
The daily awakening of the practice — the body and the land rising together, like the flower that emerges from depth and unfolds in light.
Sanskrit · padma · lotus, the flower of awakening
III
The Family’s Name
TRANSFORM · pathum
Living Well, Generation After Generation
A family name carried across generations — a standard kept not in branding, but in practice. A way of living, still lived.
Thai · patama · a family name, a long-held practice
Together, these three readings form the philosophy of PATAMA — the brand taking shape across the Montara family of properties at Trisara and Tri Vananda.
