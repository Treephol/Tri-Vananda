# PATTAMA · 15-Second Teaser · Direction 1
## Google Gemini (Veo) Production Brief · Ten Scenes

---

## DIRECTION 1 — THE NARRATIVE

**PATTAMA** · ปฐม · *paṭhama* — Thai and Pali for *first, foremost, the beginning, the original way of living.*

**Tagline.** Living Well Begins Together.
**Strapline.** The meaningful aspects of life for the multigenerational family.
**Soul.** PATTAMA is the beginning of meaningful living — wellbeing integrated into everyday life, relationships, nature, and time itself. Not escape. Living well, fully, and together.

---

## THE FILM BRIEF

**The emotional territory.** A film that feels like first light on a familiar table. Not aspirational, not aspirational-as-marketing — the quiet recognition of a life already worth living. The viewer should finish the fifteen seconds with the chest a little softer and the breath a little slower than when they started.

**The visual world.** Apichatpong Weerasethakul (*Uncle Boonmee Who Can Recall His Past Lives*) for the relationship between people and tropical place. Terrence Malick (*The Tree of Life*) for golden-hour light and reverence for ordinary moments. Hirokazu Kore-eda (*Still Walking*) for multigenerational tenderness without sentimentality. Wong Kar-wai (*In the Mood for Love*) for the camera that lingers a half-beat longer than expected. **Never:** advertising aesthetics, drone hero shots, slow-motion lifestyle clichés.

**The protagonist.** Not a product. Not a single person. The protagonist is the shared moment between generations — held, repeated, inherited.

**The structure.** Open on first light. Close on the brand name held in soft gold. What changes between them is the audience's nervous system.

**The pacing.** Read like a haiku, not like a trailer. Long enough holds that you wonder if the shot will end, then it does — exactly one beat sooner than expected.

**What this film must never be.** A travel commercial. A wellness retreat brochure. A montage of "luxury moments." A face singing into the camera. A product demo. Anything fast.

## THE TEN SCENES · GEMINI (VEO) PROMPTS

Each scene is one generation in Google Gemini (Veo). Aim for 1.3–1.5s per scene in the final cut, except Scene 10 which gets ~2s to breathe. Total = 15 seconds.

For each prompt below: paste directly into Gemini's video generation interface. Generate 3 variants per scene, select the cleanest in motion, colour, and composition, then assemble. Veo handles natural-language cinematic direction well — keep the prompts flowing rather than bullet-listed.

### Scene 01 · First Light · 1.3s
> **"Cinematic close-up shot of a single dewdrop hanging on a frangipani leaf at dawn in tropical Phuket. Soft golden-honey first light filters from behind the leaf, creating a gentle anamorphic lens flare. The leaf breathes almost imperceptibly in a barely-there morning breeze. Shallow depth of field, warm ivory and gold colour palette, 35mm film grain, the visual reverence of Terrence Malick. No people. The camera pushes in slowly and meditatively. Hyperrealistic, peaceful, unhurried."**

*Emotional beat:* the world is waking up. The beginning.

## SOUND DIRECTION

**The first 13 seconds:** ambient only. Distant birdsong. Soft wind in palms. The sound of fabric moving. The clink of one ceramic bowl on wood. A half-beat of children's distant laughter at Scene 08. Breath, never absent.

**Scenes 09–10:** a single sustained note from a *khim* (Thai hammered dulcimer) or a bowed gong, fading into a low warm pad as the typography appears. No music swell. No drum.

**Final 2 seconds (PATTAMA reveal):** the note holds, and silence returns to close.

**Never:** library music, voiceover, narration, sting, ad-style audio.

---

## PACING & ASSEMBLY NOTES

The cut should feel like turning pages of a private book, not switching channels.

- **Scenes 01–04** (5.6s) — the world wakes. Establishing.
- **Scenes 05–08** (5.8s) — the people arrive. The shared life.
- **Scene 09** (1.3s) — the inheritance.
- **Scene 10** (2.0s) — the name.

Hold every cut **exactly one frame longer** than feels comfortable. The audience should lean in. Editing rule: cut on motion or on light, never on action.

## GEMINI (VEO) GENERATION PROTOCOL

Per LUMINARY · SKILL-33 (AI-Assisted Creative Production):

1. **Generate 3 variants** of each scene prompt. Veo's output varies meaningfully across runs — never accept the first generation as final.
2. **Use Veo's "extend" function sparingly** — the brief calls for short, deliberate beats. Longer generations dilute the rhythm of the edit.
3. **Specify aspect ratio at generation time** — 16:9 master, then re-frame for 9:16 and 1:1 outputs in post (do not re-generate for vertical).
4. **Reject any output** that includes: artificial-looking faces, plastic skin texture, overly saturated colours, drone-style aerial movement, dramatic camera swoops, generic stock-footage feel, AI hallucination artefacts on hands or text.
5. **Accept only outputs** that pass the *"Does this belong specifically to PATTAMA, or could it belong to any luxury wellness brand?"* test.
6. **Hand the assembled cut** to a human colourist for a final pass — a unified warm-honey grade across all ten scenes is non-negotiable.
7. **Sound design** must be created by a human sound designer, not AI-generated.

> *Veo is the production accelerator here, not the originator. The taste, the timing, the cultural reference — these remain human work.*
