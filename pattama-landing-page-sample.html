The Masterplan

232 acres, held as a whole — the first look at the community being designed for Tri Vananda by HABITA Architects, within the Montara Wellness framework.

The community organises around a central spine — anchored by the lake, set within the regenerated forest, and held in dialogue with the existing residential cluster.

Master Plan · Zoning · 2025

The masterplan organises the new community facilities along the central spine of the property — anchored by the lake, set within the regenerated forest, and held in dialogue with the existing residential cluster.

Each building is low, biophilic, and oriented toward the trees. The community is designed within a net-zero ambition, with the seasons of Phuket setting the operational calendar.

Together, the buildings form what the brand calls PATTAMA Farm & Forest — a private membership club within nature.

Master plan — Tri Vananda · HABITA Architects with Montara Wellness, 2025. The masterplan is in development; details are subject to refinement as construction progresses.

02

The Sport Complex

A multi-discipline facility, designed for every generation.

The Sport Complex is the active heart of the community. Wood-framed structures hold racquet courts, an open-air strength and conditioning environment, indoor practice halls for Muay Thai, yoga, Tai Chi and Qigong, a 25-metre lap pool, and a children’s adventure programme woven into the property’s forest and lake systems.

Sport Complex — tennis court interior · HABITA Architects, 2025.

Wellness Centre · Interior
03

The Clinic
with a Meaningful Programme

Basic health, in conversation with longevity and traditional Thai medicine.

The Clinic is the long-arc heart of the community. Designed around a meaningful programme of preventive medicine, longevity science, and traditional Thai healing held in conversation with one another — for the practice of wellness across decades.

The Wellness Centre is built as a circular pavilion around a central courtyard pool. The Treatment Experience, the Social Recovery Lounge, and the Wellness Studio gather around the water.

Wellness Centre — interior view at the central pool · HABITA Architects, 2025.

The Meditation Hall

The pavilion at the edge of the forest — where the practice begins.

The Meditation Hall sits at the quiet edge of the community — a low wooden pavilion on stilts, oriented to the mountains, open to the morning. The room where the practice begins.

Alongside the Meditation Hall, communal pavilions, the Farm, and the property’s lakes form the connective tissue of the brand at Tri Vananda — the rooms in which the practice is shared.

Membership Package

Two tiers, one practice.

PATTAMA membership opens with two founding tiers — Belong and Bloom — taking their names from the Tri Vananda adage: Build, Belong & Bloom. Both offer year-round access to the wellness programme at Trisara, and priority access to the community facilities at Tri Vananda as they open.

I

Belong

2 Years

A first chapter of belonging — for individuals and families joining the founding cohort of PATTAMA, invited by application.

Who It Includes
  • 2 Principal Members
  • 2 Family Members
  • Multigenerational use across the immediate family
What It Opens
  • Year-round access to the wellness programme at Trisara
  • Priority access to the Tri Vananda community facilities as they open
  • Founding member standing within the brand
  • Invitations to founding-cohort gatherings and the Farm Festival
II

Bloom

5 Years

The deeper, longer chapter — for families building the practice across years, in the company of those they love most.

Who It Includes
  • 2 Principal Members
  • 2 Family Members
  • Up to 5 extended family members across two generations
What It Opens
  • All Belong tier benefits, extended across five years
  • Year-round access to all PATTAMA venues at Trisara and Tri Vananda
  • Curator privileges within the community
  • Founding member standing for the duration of the term
  • First refusal on residence enquiries at Tri Vananda

Founding membership is by application. Specific pricing and terms are available on request. Build, Belong & Bloom is the Tri Vananda adage — the path of commitment from first visit, to year-round practice, to a multigenerational home.

Location
